🔒 Privacy & Payment Security Commitment

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. All payments are processed directly through Stripe via encrypted, tokenized channels compliant with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. We do not store or have access to your full payment card number or CVV.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is strictly necessary to fulfill digital orders, deliver purchased materials, and provide responsive customer support:

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly

  • Order & Billing Details: When you purchase a digital product, you provide your full name, email address, contact phone number, and billing country/postal code.
  • Customer Inquiries: Information you provide when contacting us via email, WhatsApp, or telephone for support, including your order reference and query description.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical & Device Data: When you visit arjunyadav.com, our hosting infrastructure automatically logs standard technical metadata, including IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, date/time stamps, and pages accessed. This data is used solely for site security, debugging, and traffic analytics.
  • Cookies & Session Tokens: We use essential cookies strictly needed for site navigation, security verification, and checkout session continuity. We do not use intrusive cross-site ad tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Collected Information

We use your personal data for clear, lawful, and specific business purposes:

  • Instant Digital Delivery: To generate personalized download links and send your digital purchase access immediately to your email inbox.
  • Customer Service & Recovery: To respond to support inquiries, resend lost download links, and issue tax invoices/receipts.
  • Payment Processing & Fraud Prevention: To authenticate transactions, verify legitimate cardholder purchases, prevent payment abuse, and resolve billing disputes via Stripe.
  • Legal & Statutory Compliance: To maintain requisite accounting, tax (GST), and audit records as mandated by Indian law and RBI guidelines.

3. Parties to Whom Information Is Disclosed

We respect your privacy and will never monetize your personal data. We disclose your information only to the following strictly defined parties:

  • Authorized Payment Processors (Stripe): Your payment data is transmitted directly to Stripe Inc. and its Indian entity Stripe Payments India Pvt. Ltd. Stripe processes your transaction securely under its global PCI-DSS Level 1 certification. Stripe’s privacy practices are governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting Providers: Our web assets and databases are hosted on enterprise cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform), which implements ISO 27001, SOC 2, and SOC 3 compliant security controls.
  • Legal Authorities: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, a court of competent jurisdiction in India, or in response to a lawful inquiry by Indian law enforcement agencies.

4. Method of Disclosure & Transmission

All data shared with our trusted service providers is transmitted exclusively through authenticated, encrypted APIs utilizing Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3). No personal customer details are ever transmitted in plain, unencrypted text or over non-secure channels.

5. Security Practices & Safeguards

In adherence to Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we implement reasonable and comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS Encryption: All pages and checkout connections across arjunyadav.com are enforced with modern HTTPS encryption with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • No Card Data Storage: Neither Arjun Yadav Studio nor our web servers ever store, view, or retain your primary account number (PAN), credit/debit card numbers, CVV security codes, or banking passwords.
  • Restricted Administrative Access: Access to order databases and customer communications is strictly limited to authorized administrative personnel on secure, multi-factor authenticated devices.

6. Data Retention Policy

We retain transaction records, customer emails, and download logs only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy—including honoring customer download recovery requests, satisfying statutory accounting and taxation retention periods (generally up to 7 years for financial records under Indian tax laws), and defending against chargeback or fraudulent disputes.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Under applicable Indian data privacy regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to our statutory obligations to retain necessary taxation and financial transaction logs.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our Grievance Officer at arjunyadav.ps@gmail.com.
