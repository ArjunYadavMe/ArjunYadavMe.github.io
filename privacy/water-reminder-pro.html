💧 Pro Hydration Privacy

Water Drinking Reminder - Pro is an offline-capable daily wellness companion. All hydration tracking data, goals, and customized reminders are stored locally on your device.

1. Overview

Water Drinking Reminder - Pro ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Arjun Yadav, provides smart hydration schedules and wellness tracking. This Privacy Policy details our data commitments.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • Local Storage: All target settings, drink volumes, intervals, and historical statistics are stored locally on your device.
  • No User Registration: We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or passwords.

3. Permissions Used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to sound or display timely reminders to drink water based on your chosen schedule.
  • INTERNET: Used to display advertisements and support app features through Google AdMob.

4. Advertising

This application integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). In accordance with Google's Play Families and Advertising policies, AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to deliver relevant ads. For more details, consult Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all application history by clearing data in Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.
