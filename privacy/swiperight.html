Official Privacy Policy

SwipeRight: Card Compare India

Developer: Arjun Yadav • Package: com.creditcard.swiperight • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

🔒 Complete User Privacy

SwipeRight is an informative credit card comparison utility. We do NOT collect credit card numbers, CVVs, PINs, or bank account credentials.

1. Overview

SwipeRight: Card Compare India ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Arjun Yadav, provides Indian credit card reward calculations, cashback comparisons, and fee structures. This policy outlines our data handling practices.

2. Data Handling

  • Card Comparison Preferences: Your selected cards, spending categories, and filter preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • No Financial Credentials: The app does not ask for or collect sensitive card numbers, bank logins, OTPs, or CVV codes.
  • Offline Functionality: Card comparison tables and rewards math operate without requiring an account or network login.

3. Permissions & Third-Party SDKs

  • INTERNET: Used to update credit card fee charts and reward offers from public sources, and serve non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • AdMob Advertising: The app displays ads through Google AdMob, which may collect pseudonymous device identifiers to deliver relevant ads under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All user favorites and comparison history reside solely on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all saved preferences immediately.
