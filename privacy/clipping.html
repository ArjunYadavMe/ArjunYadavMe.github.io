Privacy Policy

Clipping: AI Video & Image Clipper

Application: com.video.clipping • Developer: Arjun Yadav • Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

Clipping is an AI-powered offline video editor, highlight detector, and background removal application developed by Arjun Yadav. We respect your privacy: all video processing, AI highlight detection, and image segmentation execute strictly on your device. None of your videos, photos, or audio recordings are uploaded to external cloud servers.

2. Data Collection & Processing

Local Processing Only: All video files, images, and audio tracks selected by you remain on your local device. We do not store, view, or transmit your media content.

Third-Party Services: The application integrates trusted industry-standard SDKs for monetization and billing:

  • Google AdMob: Serves contextual and personalized advertising. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and interaction statistics. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Audience Network: Participates in real-time in-app bidding mediation. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles secure transactions for the Pro Lifetime upgrade. Payment credentials are processed directly by Google Play.

3. Device Permissions

  • Photos / Media / Storage: Required solely to read selected videos and save exported clips to your gallery.
  • Internet Access: Required to query Google Play in-app purchases, initialize AdMob ads, and sync remote configuration.
