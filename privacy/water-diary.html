Official Privacy Policy

Water Diary: Water Drinking Reminder

Developer: Arjun Yadav • Package: com.waterdiary.drinkreminder • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

💧 100% Offline & Private Hydration Tracking

Water Diary stores all your daily intake targets, glass logs, and hydration histories strictly on your local device. We do not sell or transmit your personal health logs.

1. Overview

Water Diary: Water Drinking Reminder ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Arjun Yadav, is designed to help users establish healthy hydration habits. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Process

  • Hydration Logs: Daily water intake targets, drink volumes, timestamps, and historical streaks are saved locally in private app storage.
  • No Cloud Sync Required: The app functions entirely offline without requiring registration, account creation, or phone numbers.

3. Permissions Used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to trigger gentle local reminders throughout the day to drink water according to your custom schedule.
  • INTERNET: Used to display standard banner and interstitial ads via Google AdMob to support ongoing development.

4. Third-Party Advertising

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device advertising IDs in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Deletion

All data is stored locally. You can reset your hydration records in app settings or uninstall the app to instantly delete all data from your device.
