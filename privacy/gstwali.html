Privacy Policy for GSTWali

App: GSTWali: GSTR-1 JSON Generator  |  Package: com.arjunyadav.gstr1meesho  |  Effective Date: October 2, 2026

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment: Your Tax Data Stays Yours

GSTWali parses sales reports on-device and syncs return summaries to your private authenticated Firebase account. We never sell, rent, or monetize your business or personal tax filings.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Arjun Yadav ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the GSTWali: GSTR-1 JSON Generator mobile application and web service (collectively, "the Service").

2. Information We Collect

To provide automated GSTR-1 JSON generation and multi-platform sync, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication tokens provided when signing in via Google Sign-In or Email/Password.
  • Business Profiles: GSTIN, business trade name, and state code saved by you to organize your returns.
  • Sales & Filing Summaries: Aggregated summary metrics (turnover, tax payable, HSN count, invoice counts) saved to your authenticated account for return history and multi-device access.
  • Subscription Status: Purchase receipts and entitlement status processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing and RevenueCat.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized crash logs and basic device identifiers to ensure app stability and debug parsing errors.

3. How We Process Sales Reports & Files

When you select an Excel or CSV sales report (such as Meesho Tax Report, Amazon MTR, or Flipkart Sales Report), the parsing and calculation logic is performed directly on your device. The generated government-compliant GSTR-1 JSON file is saved locally to your device's storage for manual upload to the GST portal (gst.gov.in).

4. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with trusted third-party SDKs that process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore): Secure user authentication and encrypted cloud synchronization across web and mobile. (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: Secure processing of Pro subscription memberships without storing raw credit card or banking details. (RevenueCat Privacy)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Aggregated, non-identifying telemetry to measure feature adoption and app stability.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data:

  • You can delete individual business profiles or return summaries directly within the app at any time.
  • To request complete account and data deletion, you may delete your account from the Settings tab or email support@gstwali.in. All associated records will be permanently purged from our Firebase database within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

GSTWali is designed for businesses, e-commerce sellers, and tax professionals. It is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security Safeguards

All data transmitted between the app and cloud services uses TLS 1.3 encryption. Cloud records are protected by strict Firestore Security Rules ensuring users can only read and write their own data.
