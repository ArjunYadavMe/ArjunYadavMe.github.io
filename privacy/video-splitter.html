Official Privacy Policy

Auto Video Splitter for Status

Developer: Arjun Yadav • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview

Auto Video Splitter for Status ("we", "our", or "the app"), created by Arjun Yadav, allows users to split long videos into 30-second clips for WhatsApp status and social stories. We respect your privacy and process all media locally on your device.

2. Video & Media Handling

100% Local Processing: Videos selected for splitting are processed entirely offline on your device using hardware-accelerated local media decoders. We do NOT upload, store, or transmit your personal videos, photos, or audio recordings to any external server or cloud service.

3. Permissions Used

  • Photos / Media Access: Required solely to allow you to select a video from your gallery and save the split clips back to your gallery.
  • INTERNET: Used to display advertisements and deliver push announcements via Google AdMob and OneSignal.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use Google AdMob for monetization and OneSignal for app updates and notifications. These third-party SDKs adhere to standard privacy and data safety guidelines.
