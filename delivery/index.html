All products and services offered on arjunyadav.com are digital goods (electronic PDF publications, digital toolkits, code packages, and spreadsheets). We do not ship physical goods, printed books, or parcels. Consequently, there are no postal shipping delays or shipping fees.

1. Delivery Method & Fulfillment Process

Immediately after your payment is successfully authorized through our secure checkout with Stripe:

  • Instant Browser Access: You are automatically redirected to an immediate order confirmation and download page where you can download your PDF files, guides, and templates directly to your device.
  • Email Confirmation & Backup Link: Within 1 to 5 minutes of payment completion, an automated email is dispatched to the email address entered during checkout. This email contains your official purchase receipt, tax invoice, and permanent download links.

2. Delivery Timeline

Electronic delivery is instantaneous. Under normal network conditions, digital download access is available within seconds. If you have completed payment but do not see your delivery email within 10 minutes:

  1. Check your email's Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.
  2. Ensure your inbox is not over storage quota.
  3. If you still cannot locate it, contact our domestic support desk at arjunyadav.ps@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 85819 55657. We will manually verify your payment and re-send your files within 2 to 4 business hours.

3. Delivery Costs & Taxes

Because all products are delivered electronically via internet transmission, there are zero shipping charges, courier fees, or packaging costs anywhere in India or internationally. All prices shown on product pages represent the complete digital product price.

4. Re-downloading & Lost Files

We understand that devices may be replaced or files accidentally misplaced. If you need to re-download a previously purchased product:

  • Simply click the download link in your original confirmation email.
