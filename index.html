05 / BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions you
may have.

Something else on your mind?
Just ask me ↗

Can I explain what I need in Hindi?+

Yes. You can call or WhatsApp me in Hindi or English. आप अपनी ज़रूरत हिंदी में बता सकते हैं।

I don’t know whether I need a website or an app.+

That’s fine. Tell me about your business and what customers or staff need to do. We’ll discuss a suitable starting point before you decide.

Can I start with just one service?+

Absolutely. We can focus on your Google Business Profile, a website, or an app. If a smaller improvement solves the problem, that’s a good place to start.

How much will my project cost?+

It depends on the work involved. After a short discussion, I’ll provide a written scope and quote. Domain names, hosting, paid services, revisions and ongoing support are explained before you commit.

Do you guarantee the first position on Google?+

No. Local ranking depends on several factors, including relevance, distance and prominence. I focus on accurate information and agreed improvements. Your business retains ownership of its profile.

What do you need from me to get started?+

A short description of your business and what you want to improve. For delivery, we’ll agree on content, genuine photos, approvals and any required access. For Google profiles, you can invite me as a manager without sharing your password.

Can we work together if I’m outside Ranchi?+

Yes. We can discuss and review projects remotely. If a project needs an in-person visit, we’ll agree on availability and arrangements first.

What happens after launch?+

Your proposal sets out the handover, included support period and any optional maintenance. You’ll know which accounts and materials you receive and what future changes would cost.
