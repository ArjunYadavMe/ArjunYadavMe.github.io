Refunds & digital delivery

This is a digital ebook and practice pack. No physical item is shipped. The files are PDFs and require a PDF reader.

Delivery

The hosted checkout provides access after confirmed payment. If access fails, contact arjunyadav.ps@gmail.com with your order reference and checkout email. Please do not send passwords, OTPs or full card details.

Refund requests

For the launch, you may request a refund within seven days of purchase by emailing the support address. Include the order reference and the email used at checkout. We aim to reply within two business days.

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment provider. The time until funds appear depends on that provider. If there is a duplicate charge, faulty file or non-delivery, contact support promptly so it can be investigated.

Applicable consumer rights remain unaffected. The final customer price is shown at checkout.
