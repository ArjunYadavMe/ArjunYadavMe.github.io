⚡ Instant Digital Products Notice

All products sold on arjunyadav.com are digital goods (such as PDF ebooks, downloadable guides, spreadsheet systems, and code kits). Because digital assets are delivered instantly and cannot be physically returned or revoked once downloaded, purchases are generally final once the digital files have been accessed.

1. Conditions Under Which Refunds Are Granted

Notwithstanding the irrevocable nature of downloadable digital files, Arjun Yadav Studio provides 100% full refunds under the following specific circumstances:

1. Duplicate Transaction

If you were accidentally billed more than once for a single order due to a network glitch, slow connectivity, or double-click at checkout, we will refund the duplicate charge in full immediately.

2. Technical Non-Delivery

If you did not receive your download link and our support team is unable to provide working download access or backup files within 48 hours of your notification, you are entitled to a 100% full refund.

3. Defective or Corrupted Files

If the delivered digital file is corrupted or technically defective, and our team cannot rectify the issue with a compatible working replacement within 48 hours, a full refund will be granted.

4. Pre-Download Accidental Purchase

If an order was made by mistake and our server logs verify that the download link has not been opened or clicked, you may request a cancellation within 2 hours of purchase for a complete refund.

2. Situations Not Eligible for Refunds

To prevent fraudulent exploitation and piracy, refunds will not be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Change of Mind: We do not offer refunds simply because you changed your mind after downloading and reading the content. Free sample pages and comprehensive descriptions are provided on product pages prior to purchase.
  • Lack of Technical Tools: Our digital files are delivered in universal, standard formats (PDF, CSV, HTML, ZIP). It is the customer's responsibility to possess a standard device or application capable of viewing standard files.
  • Unauthorized Redistribution: Any customer found violating our Terms of Service by reselling or publicly leaking proprietary materials forfeits all rights to refund or support.

3. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To request a refund under one of the qualifying conditions above, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our support desk at arjunyadav.ps@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request — [Your Order ID]".
  2. Include the following details:
    • Your full name
    • The email address used during purchase
    • Stripe transaction ID or invoice reference number
    • Reason for the refund request (with screenshots if reporting a technical glitch)

Our team will acknowledge and review your request within 24 hours.

4. Refund Method & Processing Timeline

Once a refund is approved by our support team:

  • Initiation: The refund is triggered immediately through our Stripe dashboard.
  • Original Payment Method: All refunds are issued exclusively back to the original source used at checkout (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI). For security and anti-money laundering reasons, we do not issue cash or alternate third-party account payouts.
  • Bank Processing Window: The funds will appear on your bank statement within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your card issuer or banking institution's standard clearance timeline.
  • Confirmation: An automated Stripe refund receipt will be emailed to your purchase address as soon as the refund is initiated.
